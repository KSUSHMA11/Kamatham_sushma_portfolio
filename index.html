Who I Am

I'm Kamatham Sushma, an AI Training Data Specialist & Quality Control Engineer based in Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh, India. I specialise in creating high-quality, labelled datasets that power the next generation of AI and computer vision models.

At DreamVu Pvt. Ltd., Hyderabad, I began as an Annotation Team member and was Promoted to QC Engineer — a recognition of my precision, consistency, and deep understanding of AI data quality standards.

My work revolves around video annotation, bounding box labeling, object tracking, and frame-level quality review — ensuring every data point meets strict accuracy standards before it trains a model.

When I'm away from the screen, you'll find me traveling, clicking photographs, or watching a good documentary.
